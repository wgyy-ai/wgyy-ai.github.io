# BreastStage

## Dataset Information

BreastStage is a multimodal breast-cancer-centered dataset referenced by the BreastGPT work and associated BreastStage-Bench evaluation suite. Public descriptions indicate that it integrates 17 sub-datasets across 5 imaging modalities and reorganizes heterogeneous evidence around the clinical workflow of breast cancer care, including screening, diagnosis, and treatment planning.

The publicly referenced release notes indicate that some components derived from institutional clinical data may be subject to IRB, de-identification, or data-use restrictions, while metadata, task definitions, evaluation code, and access instructions are intended for release.
