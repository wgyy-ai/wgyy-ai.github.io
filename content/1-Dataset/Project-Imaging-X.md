# Project-Imaging-X

## Dataset Information

Project-Imaging-X is a survey-and-collection style resource from the General Medical AI team focused on open-access medical imaging datasets for foundation model development. The associated paper describes it as covering more than 1,000 open-access medical imaging datasets and advocating a metadata-driven fusion paradigm for large-scale medical foundation data construction.

Unlike a conventional single training set, the Hugging Face repository mainly packages survey materials and metadata resources, including a PDF report and supporting assets.
