# Med-MAT

## Dataset Information

Med-MAT is a medical multimodal assessment dataset hosted by FreedomIntelligence. The Hugging Face page exposes image-grounded question answering records with both direct-answer and multiple-choice variants, and links the dataset to arXiv:2412.20070. The public preview shows fields such as `image`, `Question`, `Answer`, `Question-choice`, and `Answer-choice`, indicating its use for visual multiple-choice medical reasoning.

The repository also exposes two organizational views: `Aggregated_Subsets` and `Original_Medical_Datasets`, suggesting that Med-MAT is an aggregated benchmark assembled from multiple underlying medical image datasets rather than a single-source dataset.
