# ChiMed-VL

## Dataset Information

ChiMed-VL is a Chinese medical vision-language dataset with two major components: an alignment subset and an instruction subset. The dataset card reports 580,014 image-text pairs in `ChiMed-VL-Alignment` and 469,441 QA pairs in `ChiMed-VL-Instruction`, for a total of 1,049,455 multimodal records.

The public preview shows bilingual biomedical figure captions and LLaVA-style conversational data, indicating that the resource is designed for Chinese medical multimodal pretraining and instruction tuning.
